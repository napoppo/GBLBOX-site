The numbers that decide the fight.

SCP (overall bulk)

Attack, Defense, and HP in one number. Weigh damage against staying power, per league.

Actual Attack, Defense, HP

What the stats become once you power up. How many hits you survive comes from here, not a percentage.

Reachable level and CP

How far you can power up under the CP cap, with the stardust and candy it takes.

Breakpoints

Work back from the line where a move lands one more damage to the Attack value you need.

Questions

Can GBLBOX check Pokémon GO IVs from screenshots?

Yes. Load the appraisal screenshot and you get IVs, CP, HP, and each league rank.

What is SCP?

Overall bulk: the actual Attack, Defense, and HP values multiplied together. Useful when two ranks are close.

Which leagues does GBLBOX support?

Great, Ultra, and Master: ranks, stat product, SCP, stat values, evolutions.

Is screenshot analysis safe?

Normal image analysis runs on your device; nothing is sent to an outside service.
