About Pokémon GO IV checks and GBLBOX

Can GBLBOX check Pokémon GO IVs from screenshots?

Yes. Save the appraisal screen as a screenshot and use GBLBOX to check IVs, CP, HP, and league ranks.

Which PvP leagues does GBLBOX support?

GBLBOX helps you check PvP ranks and stats for Great League, Ultra League, and Master League.

Is screenshot analysis private?

Normal image analysis runs on your device, so you can review screenshots without sending them to an external analysis service.
