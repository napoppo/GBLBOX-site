The numbers that decide the fight.

SCP (overall bulk)

Attack, Defense, and HP combined into one number. Check it alongside rank and stat product when you want to weigh damage against staying power. Calculated per league.

Actual Attack, Defense, HP

What the stats become once you power up. When you are counting how many hits you survive, this is the number to read, not a percentage.

Reachable level and CP

How far you can power up under the CP cap. The stardust and candy required are listed too, so the cost is part of the decision.

Breakpoints

Work backwards from the line where a move lands one more point of damage to the Attack value you need. You can see whether you are just short.

Questions

Can GBLBOX check Pokémon GO IVs from screenshots?

Yes. Load the appraisal screenshot into GBLBOX to check IVs, CP, HP, and each league rank.

What is SCP?

A measure of overall bulk: the actual Attack, Defense, and HP values multiplied together. GBLBOX calculates it per league so you can compare individuals whose ranks are close.

Which leagues does GBLBOX support?

Great, Ultra, and Master League ranks, stat product, SCP, actual stat values, and evolution results.

Is screenshot analysis safe?

Normal image analysis runs on your device. You can check IVs from screenshots without sending them to an outside service.
